# TownOfNDev

**TownOfNDev** is an extension mod for **Town of Us: Mira**, developed by **NDev Studios**.

It expands Town of Us with a collection of custom roles and modifiers designed to introduce new investigative, protective, killing, neutral, and utility mechanics while remaining integrated with the existing Town of Us: Mira experience.

> TownOfNDev is currently in **Beta**.

## Roles

### Crewmate

#### Tracer
**Alignment:** Crewmate Investigative

Use Dust to mark players and follow the traces they leave behind.

#### SUI
**Alignment:** Crewmate Protective

Protect other players and gain information when your protection is triggered.

#### Tank
**Alignment:** Crewmate Protective

Complete all of your tasks to become protected against ordinary direct kill attempts.

---

### Impostor

#### Condemner
**Alignment:** Impostor Killing

Mark players for condemnation and use the Death Note to control who is placed on Death Row.

#### Reverser
**Alignment:** Impostor Power

Activate Alert to temporarily reverse direct attacks back onto the attacker.

---

### Neutral

#### Fungi
**Alignment:** Neutral Evil

Spread an infection through the crew and work towards your own unique win condition.

#### Troll
**Alignment:** Neutral Evil

Cause disruption and work towards the Troll's unique objective.

## Installation

TownOfNDev requires a compatible installation of **Town of Us: Mira** and its required dependencies.

1. Install Town of Us: Mira.
2. Download the latest TownOfNDev DLL from the [Releases](../../releases) page.
3. Place the TownOfNDev DLL alongside the appropriate BepInEx/Town of Us plugin files.
4. Launch Among Us.

For beta builds, make sure all players are using compatible versions of the required mods.
